# Heel & Steel

**The fantasy RPG you play by walking.**

Your hero takes on quests at the pub, and your real steps carry them through. Walk to finish quests, earn gold and gear, fight your way down the Cellar, brawl other players' heroes, and walk together with your pub table each week.

- Quests powered by your real steps, from your iPhone, Apple Watch and fitness apps
- Bosses, loot and a hero to build
- Brawls, a leaderboard and pub tables with friends
- Your walk live on the lock screen

**Coming to the iPhone App Store.** Friends are testing it on TestFlight first; join the [Telegram group](https://t.me/heelandsteel) to hear when it is out.
