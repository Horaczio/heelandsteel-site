# Support

**Heel & Steel: Walking RPG**

## Contact

Email [support@heelandsteel.com](mailto:support@heelandsteel.com) for problems, questions, account and data requests, and appeals. Please include your hero's name and the app version (at the bottom of Settings).

Community: [t.me/heelandsteel](https://t.me/heelandsteel). Do not post account details there; admins never message you first to ask for passwords or codes ([chat rules](/chat-rules/#telegram)).

## Your data and your account {#account}

### Get a copy of your data

In the game, Settings > Download my data, or email us.

### Delete your account {#delete-account}

In the game, Settings > Delete my hero and account. Everything is deleted at once (and from the server's backups within 7 days). Without the app, email [support@heelandsteel.com](mailto:support@heelandsteel.com) with the subject "Delete my account", from the email address your account uses or with your hero's name; we delete it within a month and confirm.

More in the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Playing safely

Steps count with the phone in your pocket and the app closed: you never need to look at the game while walking. Watch where you are going, follow traffic rules, and walk only as much as is right for you. See [terms of use](/terms/#walk-safely).
